On the mechanical worry — whether varying the rate can actually produce the effect rather than just being a clever idea on paper. The thing that makes it work isn't rhythm tricks, it's withholding the orientation paragraph. Every chapter of every novel opens by telling you where and when you are; it's so conventional you don't see it.
