the first time i smoked i smoked to escape. to escape pain.

but i thought i had it figured out. pain wasnt physical pain, nerve pain, damage to body - signals from nevres warning pressure or temprature or even hunger, pain was anguish - loss, loneliness, rejection, jealousy, fear, hate, the things that damage the mind.

but that's not the true pain.

i found out too late.

so i lunged for the sparkling crystal that had fascinated me for so long - about 6 inches in length, a bubble in the form of a skull with an opening in the forhead, the neck leading to a spirallng tube, looping 6 times before straightening out to a mouthpiece.

in the bubble shaped bowl was a tiny ball of bright orange waxy resin - it had a penetrating, medicinal and woody smell.

i knew what to do, i'd seen it done many times - hold the bowl over the candle flame, not so close it formed soot on the bowl and got too hot too fast, not so far it didnt melt the resin.
it wasn't as easyas it looked - the candle flame flicked even down here in wet where air moved the least - this constantly changed the size of the flame, thence the distance it was from the bowl as well as moving it off center.

nevertheless, the tiny dot of resin softened and deformed, then began to liquify, and gradually became a bright orange dot of oil from which the purest white fumes arose.
i started to inhale as if it were the sweet, fruity hou po so beloved of the moths. i should have known better - this was like trying to breathe the chapel censors, thick, cloying and mediciney - but it also opened my lungs in a kind of cooling way, like it wanted to be inside me, to fill me.

it streamed off the vapourising oil in the bowl, spiralled round the coil, turning the sides bright pure opaque white as it condensed on the cool lass and poured into my lungs. incrediby dense for such a small blob of resin, it went in so fast and deep i choked and coughed.

there was a rich chuckle from the others - "told ya you'd cough newbie, everyone does first time. it smokes you, you just can't compare it."

but even as the words rang in my ears my vision faded and i sank deep into darkness.

above, near the ceiling in the darkness a lone moth fluttered a way from the fumes - this should have been the sign that warned us all, but of course not a soul noticed.

they were all watching me.

we thought she was just another wetpunk kid - old enough to escape from her troubles and looking for something new.

hair cut asymmetric and shaved on one side/round the back, below the ear, combed over from ontop on the other, dyed bright glowmold green. she seemed unremarkable, dressed in the usual bioplastics and other anti-rot stuff cultured by the wetfolk. mostly dark colours and cut kinda sharp - no excess, straight edges - no cuffs or folds to trap moisture dripping and flowing and poling everywhere.

but as she inhaled she seemed hit fast, way too fast, sinking back into a slump the pipe dropped into her lap before yoshihiro could even try to catch it - no-one even had a chance to notice the hot pipe singeing the cloth.

all the glomold in the room begins to shift to dull red and pulse.

suddenly she arched backward, upward and let out the most unearthly sound i ever heard - no human ever made a sound like this, it was deep and haunting and every hair on my bdy stood on end. it terrified me to the core, without me even processing a thought, on a deeply primal level.

suddenly she started to seize - i had never seen a grand mal seizure but yoshihiro was old enough to have a little knowledge - he grabbed the pipe, swept away the candle and tea stuff off the low side table and pulled it out of range in a panic.

we all were frozen as she convulsed violently - no-one spoke, no-one acted, no-one even ran for help. what could we do? it was too late to stop it now and we coudnt move her whilst she was thrashing round like this. of course no-one wanted to tell the doorsmen or even agri - too many questions would be asked.

so we all sat dumbfounded and terrified, transfixed as for what seemed an eternity, watching her bend and contort into awful positions upon the cushions. in the dim light and the slight strobe effect of the flickering candles it was a weird near silent danse macabre unaccompanied by any music heard on this plane.

the same thought was predominant in everyones head - what if she dies, or maybe worse what if she never comes out , remains a vegetable for ever - who will tell her family, who will face that brute of a father - the man was the biggest guy in the wet, he terrified everyone and he had a temper - plus he was powerful, he could make us suffer, and really drag it out.

maybe 15 minutes passed before the seizure subsided but she didnt wake - she remained slumped, unconcious at first. whilst some of the group started hurriedly collecting paraphenalia, drugs, cleaning up foils and bags, guiltily hiding all evidence, only me and yoshihiro attended to the girl.

glomold gradually rreturns to the sual yellow-green light.

by the time she started to gradually open her eyes and mumble near an hour had passed and we had gotten her sitting propped up on cushions.

"what do we do? take her to agri or the brewers?"

"neither - she'll be ok, she's waking up, she just needs time, we can't tell anyone" 

"what about the others?"

"don't worry about them, they're too scared of sumo - none of them will sa..."

this was when she started to speak. but we couldnt make out what she was saying. it wasn't common and it wasn't wetspeak. it sounded kinda like wetspeak but with more... harsh, clicky consonant sounds and kinda glottal, deep throat vowels that were near non-human - unlike any other citadel language we recognised - even those of the our most powerful secret - the wetfolk's friends in the other citadels.

yoshihiro looked distinctly spooked but i was fascinated more than scared by now - that lifelong curiosity that always drove me overtaking any fear of the unknown.

yoshiro exclaimed suddenly, "fucking skysnakes, look at her eyes!"

he was right - the irises glowed, but not glomold green or the orangey-yellow of a candle - this was a dark red. there was fucking light coming from her eyes.

as fast as we could begin to react she sat bolt upright and exhaled sharple then slumped down again and ban to cry silently, tears falling one by one down her high cheek bones.
