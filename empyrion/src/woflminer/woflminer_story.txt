Woflcorp. Military & Industrial: Miner [Warp] - The Prospectors' Lifeline

Log Entry: Oort Expanse | Sector 88-K | Asteroid Field 42

Pilot: Specialist Devrik JunoUnit: Woflcorp. Miner [Warp]Mission: Deep Space Mining & Prospecting Operations

Space wasn’t empty.

Not really. Not when you knew where to look.

Devrik Juno had spent most of his working life in the dark expanse, chasing resource veins through asteroid fields, drilling through frozen rock, and hunting for the rarest of materials. His ship, the Woflcorp. Miner [Warp], was his livelihood.

Six T2 laser drill modules hummed softly as he guided the ship through a chaotic mess of spinning rock. His HUD mapped the belt in real-time, highlighting the best extraction points. A particularly rich cluster of Tungstate-V deposits pulsed on his scanner—a rare find, and one that would fetch a fortune back at Outpost Velka.

Juno smirked. "Let’s dig."

Drilling Through the Void

The hex-patterned Tough-Grid™ high-impact ablative armor was built to take a beating. Even if a rock came loose and slammed into the hull, it’d bounce off harmlessly. That gave Juno the confidence to push the vessel deeper into the asteroid’s surface, cutting through solid rock like it was butter.

The six laser drills fired simultaneously, their synchronized pulses blasting apart the rock. Chunks disintegrated into dust, and his twin harvest storage containers quickly filled with rich ore.

He flipped the stabilizers to micro-adjust mode, allowing the ship’s 360-degree thruster design to make near-impossible adjustments in close quarters. The Miner [Warp] wasn’t just a tool—it was a scalpel.

Unexpected Visitors

A proximity alert blared through his cockpit.

Something wasn’t right.

Juno switched from drill cam to tactical display. A second vessel had just entered the belt. It was unregistered, running without an ID tag.

Pirates. Of course.

Evac Protocol

Mining ships weren’t built for combat. But they weren’t defenseless, either.

Juno powered down the drills and flicked a switch. The warp core warmed up, feeding energy into the short-range warp drive. He’d have one shot to jump clear before things got ugly.

The pirate vessel closed the distance fast, its forward thrusters glowing red-hot. Probably packing EMP charges to disable his systems before boarding.

Juno’s hands flew over the controls. A rapid maneuver, flipping the ship into a vertical ascent, bought him seconds. He ejected his harvested ore into space—a distraction. The pirates veered toward the floating cargo, hungry for an easy prize.

Big mistake.

Juno punched the warp drive. A bright flash engulfed his vision as the Miner [Warp] blinked into FTL.

Mission Log: Successful Extraction

Destination: Outpost Velka.

The Miner [Warp] emerged on the far side of the system, safely outside the pirate-infested belt. He leaned back, exhaling. Another haul secured. Another near-miss.

Juno tapped the ship’s console, a grin creeping across his face.

“Good job, old girl. Let’s do it again tomorrow.”

End Transmission.
