Survival features

Inside it has ? volume storage, ? volume ammo storage, o², wifi, detector and long range detector .


Offence and Defence

The ship comes with 4 railguns and 2 turrets. there's a shield with 3x chargers for covering your back whilst you get out of ot water as fast as possible.
- There are twin armoured cockpits sunk into the front armour making them a bit harder to hit.
- Hull material is... engines - there is no monocoque hull, instead the components form the framework and the ship is only made of separate steel plating where ablative armour is present.
- The ship is designed to take some hits and does part separation/redundancy as well as an additional front layer of protection.
- [note the shield charger spaces are filled with large generators in the saved ship because i didn't have chargers available in the creative mode i was using - they are painted a distinctive bright green and left accessible to aid the switch].
- It can even be used to take on light POI's but expect some damage from hits.


Performance

- all engine design - no hull design saves weight immensely - engines attach to engines around a central armoured core.
- and at that, there's two of the biggest engines in production just for forward thrust - and the rest of the ship is built to match!
- special hybrid alien tech Levit8™ Aero Ablative armour aerodynamic shaping reduces drag.
- much fuels.
