The woflcorp. Forestry Unit 1a is an ultra-low cost forestry harvester/clearance machine for gen purpose tree felling designed to work with alpha 10 requirements. The vessel is at 4k CPU use and requires unlock level of 12.

========================================================


Survival features

- Inside it has ? harvest container for wood storage but therefore could be used for any small amount of storage if needed.
- also present are is a detector and great utility spotlighting.
- very cheap to build [if built as tier 1 CPU for no upgrade version is extreme budget version indeed]


Offence and Defence

- Although the vessel comes with no active offensive armament or armour/shielding, it is very capable of achieving high speeds if evacuation of an area becomes necessary.
- There is an armoured cockpit sunk low into the front for maximum streamlining and also making it somewhat harder to hit.
- Hull material is a pure carbon fibre monocoque hull existing to channel air for cooling and channelling resources into the machinery with minimum snags, [providing little defence in the form of armour against most weapons].
- The vessel is not designed to take hits and although there is a degree of part separation/redundancy, it mostly achieves any defensive capability by speed.


Performance

- extreme lightweight carbon fibre low profile hull design saves weight immensely - engines are present in number sufficient to provide high speed and acceleration when combined with low weight/drag.
- the carbon fibre skin is extremely low profile hence provides some degree of aerodynamic shaping, hence reducing drag.
- greatly reduced fuel consumption results allowing greater range, runtime and also room for resource storage.
