Woflcorp. Forestry Unit 1a: The Cutter’s Edge

Log Entry: Site Theta-Prime | Outer Inzaka-4 Woodlands

Operator: Davin "Blitz" CorvoUnit ID: Forestry Unit 1a-07Mission: Resource Extraction & Land Clearance

The dense alien jungle stretched endlessly in all directions, its towering blackwood trees swaying against the eerie, fog-laced horizon of Inzaka-4. Somewhere beyond the tree line, buried in the wilds, lay the wreckage of a lost Woflcorp. survey vessel—a classified payload rumored to hold prototype atmospheric stabilizers.

Davin “Blitz” Corvo wasn’t here for classified salvage, though.

His job was simple: clear a path, extract resources, and don’t ask too many questions.

The Forestry Unit 1a rumbled beneath him, its ultra-light carbon fiber chassis barely making a sound as it hovered over the uneven terrain. The low-profile hull kept the dust down, while the oversized thrusters gave him enough speed to bolt if things went south. With his harvest container prepped and the saws calibrated, Blitz toggled his comms.

“Control, this is Blitz. I’m on-site. Starting the clearing op now.”

The First Cut

The Forestry Unit’s carbon-alloy saws roared to life, bladed arms unfolding like the mandibles of a beast. The first tree fell in seconds—its massive trunk sheared through effortlessly, the remains sucked into the storage bay for processing. Blitz swept the controls methodically, pushing deeper into the jungle, leaving a trail of neatly processed blackwood in his wake.

At first, it was just another job.

Then the detector pinged.

Blitz frowned, tapping at his console. The scanner had caught something—anomalous readings, faint but unmistakable. A weak distress signal. It wasn’t Woflcorp. frequency.

“Control, I got something weird here,” he muttered. “Not on our grid.”

A pause. Then, static-laced response:

“Stay on task, Blitz. No deviations.”

Typical Woflcorp.

Blitz exhaled sharply, tapping the thrusters. “Yeah, yeah…”

But he was already turning toward the signal.

The Discovery

Navigating through the underbrush, Blitz steered the Forestry Unit around a cluster of thick, gnarled roots, keeping his eyes on the sensor feed. The distress beacon grew stronger. Whatever was out there had been lost a long time.

Then he saw it.

A downed Falcon-class runner, half-buried under the thick jungle foliage, its hull scorched and punctured. A long-dead beacon still flickered in the dark, its weak signal the last remnant of a failed escape.

Blitz killed the saws and drifted closer, keeping his hand near the ejection throttle in case he needed a fast exit. Woflcorp. had its share of enemies. He didn’t want to be mistaken for one of them.

He popped the external speaker. “Anyone alive in there?”

Silence.

Then—a scraping sound from inside the wreck.

Blitz stiffened.

Something moved.

Ghosts of the Past

The Forestry Unit wasn’t built for combat, but it had one advantage—speed. Blitz reversed hard, backing away as a shadow moved inside the wreckage. A figure emerged, stepping into the dim light, covered in torn pilot fatigues and grime.

They were alive.

Barely.

The pilot staggered forward, squinting at the Forestry Unit’s lights before collapsing against a tree. Blitz hit the exterior floodlights, illuminating them fully.

They were wearing a Woflcorp. insignia.

“Son of a…” Blitz muttered. He opened the canopy and leapt down. “You a ghost, or are you real?”

The pilot groaned, coughing. “Real enough… I need… evac…”

Blitz glanced at the damaged Falcon-class runner behind them, then at the dense jungle he’d carved through to get here. The nearest Woflcorp. extraction team was light-years away.

The Forestry Unit 1a wasn’t meant for search and rescue.

But it had more thrust than it needed, extreme fuel efficiency, and just enough space in the harvest storage to carry someone out of hell.

Blitz sighed, already knowing this wasn’t going to sit well with command.

“Well, buddy, hope you don’t mind riding with the cargo.”

The pilot barely managed a weak nod before passing out.

Run or Die

Blitz wasted no time. He strapped the pilot into the cargo bay’s side alcove, dumped half the processed wood to reduce weight, and spun the unit back toward the clearing.

His instincts screamed trouble was coming.

As if to confirm it, his scanner blared with an incoming signature.

Not Woflcorp.

Not friendly.

Blitz punched the throttle. The Forestry Unit shot forward, its lightweight hull cutting through the jungle like a phantom. A moment later, plasma fire ripped through the trees behind him.

Whoever was out there, they wanted that pilot dead.

Blitz gritted his teeth. The Forestry Unit 1a wasn’t designed for dogfights, but it could outrun almost anything on the ground. He kicked into a full-speed hover burn, the jungle blurring past in a rush of neon-lit trees and smoke.

He wasn’t just running blind, though.

He was leading them.

The original clearing zone was up ahead. If he could hit the drop-point, Woflcorp’s local security team would have just enough range to intercept the pursuers.

If he survived that long.

Final Stretch

The cockpit shook as Blitz skidded into the clearing, burning thrusters hot enough to scorch the dirt beneath him. The pilot was unconscious but still breathing. That was enough.

A distant hum cut through the air—a low, predatory sound.

Blitz turned to see a black Woflcorp. interceptor craft descending. Its cannons rotated toward the unseen pursuers in the jungle.

A voice crackled over his comms: “Forestry Unit 1a-07, we have your location. Stand by.”

A barrage of railgun fire lanced past him, shredding through the trees. The unknown pursuers vanished in the chaos.

Blitz let out a slow exhale.

The Woflcorp. security lead stepped out of the drop-ship, her gaze shifting between Blitz, the Forestry Unit, and the unconscious pilot.

“Davin Corvo, right?” she said. “Looks like you found something you weren’t supposed to.”

Blitz smirked, leaning against his battered Forestry Unit. “Yeah, well… the pay’s gonna reflect that, right?”

She chuckled, motioning for medics to retrieve the pilot.

“Woflcorp. takes care of its own,” she said, “even when they break the rules.”

Blitz glanced back at the Forestry Unit 1a—the machine that wasn’t built for combat, but had just saved a life and outrun death.

Maybe these things were tougher than they looked.

Mission Complete.

End Transmission.
