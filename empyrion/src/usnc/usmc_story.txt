“When the Giants Land”
A CVN-2018 “Indomitable” Adjacent Story

Chapter One: The Thunder Before the Storm
The people of Kestran-V had always been proud of their neutrality. Tucked away at the fringes of controlled space, their world had been untouched by the wars that burned across the core systems. They had built a world of glass towers, endless farms, and blue-water oceans. They had no standing military, no orbital defenses—just trade agreements, diplomatic pacts, and the naive belief that peace was something that could be negotiated forever.

That belief shattered in a single night.

The Razan Dominion came screaming out of the void, tearing through the colony’s comm network before their diplomats even had a chance to open a channel. The sky ignited as plasma bombardment melted through orbital stations and fire fell upon the capital. Cities burned, transport ships were cut down mid-flight, and within hours, Kestran-V was a world under siege.

A desperate distress signal slipped through the chaos, a single pulse of sound lost in the void. The citizens of Kestran-V never knew if it had reached anyone, but someone had been listening.

Three days later, the sky split apart.

Chapter Two: The Indomitable Arrives
Admiral Caine Vasquez stood at the bridge of the CVN-2018 “Indomitable,” watching the planet below through reinforced armor-glass. His hands clenched behind his back, his scarred knuckles white with fury. The Dominion was making a habit of this—razing colonies, executing governors, and leaving ruin in their wake. Not this time.

The Indomitable had dropped out of warp directly over Kestran-V’s atmosphere, its engines rumbling like the growl of an awakening god. On its flanks, two battlecruisers and a swarm of strike frigates burned their way into the Dominion blockade. Blue plasma bolts raked across the carrier’s shields, but its massive cannons answered in kind.

The Admiral’s voice was calm, heavy, inevitable.

“Deploy the Marines.”

The lower hull of the Indomitable lit up. Troop bays cycled open. Drop pods armed. Atmospheric landers primed.

In the span of thirty seconds, an entire United Systems Marine Corps battalion was in freefall.

Chapter Three: When the Giants Land
The Dominion invaders saw the fire in the sky and thought it was another orbital bombardment.

They were wrong.

Hundreds of armored drop pods slammed into the capital, each one a self-contained war machine carrying a squad of battle-hardened Marines. The moment they landed, their reinforced hatches exploded outward, and rifles opened fire before the dust could even settle.

On the outskirts of the city, the Indomitable herself touched down. The ground shook like an earthquake, glass windows in towers shattered from the force, and Dominion shock troopers found themselves staring up at the sky-blackening shadow of a warship built to bring the hammer of vengeance.

Boom. Boom. Boom.

The heavy deployment ramps slammed down, and out they came.

Tanks rolled onto the battlefield, their turrets already firing before their tracks had fully extended. Gunships screamed overhead, laying down strafing runs across enemy artillery positions. And from the yawning belly of the Indomitable, row upon row of United Systems Marines stormed into the fray.

The Dominion had expected scattered militia, desperate refugees, maybe a few civilian defense forces.

They got the entire wrath of the United Systems Marine Corps, delivered by a fortress of steel and fire.

Chapter Four: The Cost of War
Sergeant Dorian Rask kicked down the door of an enemy-controlled garrison and emptied his mag into the first Dominion officer he saw. The bastards had taken civilians hostage, herding them into the lower levels of a ruined tower. Not anymore.

“Room clear! Move, move, move!”

His squad swept forward, close-quarters firefights lighting up the darkened interior. One by one, the Dominion fell back—until they had nowhere left to go.

Rask grabbed the last one standing by the collar, slamming him against the wall.

“You enjoy shooting civilians, Dominion?”

The man didn’t answer. His eyes flicked to the window, to the battlefield outside, to the towering silhouette of the Indomitable, its heavy cannons still thundering orbital fire into distant enemy positions.

“You’re already dead,” Rask told him. “You just don’t know it yet.”

Chapter Five: After the Dust Settles
By the time the Dominion realized their mistake, it was too late. The city was secured. The survivors were freed. The enemy had no choice but to retreat.

From the bridge of the Indomitable, Admiral Vasquez watched their warships flee into the void. His comm officer turned to him.

“Orders, sir?”

Vasquez’s jaw tightened. The Dominion had been taught a lesson today. But if they thought they could just run back into the shadows—they were wrong.

“Pursue them,” he said. “And burn their fleet to the ground.”

Epilogue: The Legend of the Indomitable
Kestran-V never forgot the day the Indomitable landed. The world had burned. They had screamed for help. And in answer, the sky had torn open with fire and fury.

They told stories of the warship that walked among the ruins. Of the soldiers who fell from the heavens and fought like demons.

They told stories of a giant, forged in war, a ship whose very name became legend.

The Indomitable had come—and war had followed in its wake.

And the Dominion?

They never dared set foot on Kestran-V again.
