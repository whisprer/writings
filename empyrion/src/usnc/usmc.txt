USNC CVN-2018 “Indomitable” – Marine Corps Assault Carrier
"Send a message they’ll never forget."

A looming titan of steel and fire, the CVN-2018 “Indomitable” is the Marine Corps’ answer to deep-space planetary incursions and full-scale planetary warfare. Designed as a troop carrier, orbital assault platform, and mobile garrison, it carries the weight of entire campaigns on its heavily-armored hull.

Built for shock deployment, the CVN-2018 can deliver thousands of Marines, armored vehicles, and gunships straight into the heart of battle. Its reinforced drop bays line the lower hull, allowing simultaneous deployment of multiple strike teams through rapid-launch troop pods and vehicle deployment bays.

Firepower & Defensive Systems
While primarily a troop carrier, the Indomitable is no helpless transport—it is a brutal warship in its own right.

Multiple twin-linked heavy railguns and rotary cannons provide devastating orbital fire support.
Autonomous anti-fighter turrets ensure no enemy vessel gets too close.
Reinforced armor plating and kinetic-dispersive shielding allow it to withstand sustained enemy fire, making it a floating fortress.
Dual-pattern missile silos allow strategic bombardment from orbit or planetary surface defenses suppression.
Deployment & Logistics
Drop Pod & Lander Bays: A vast internal hangar accommodates a full marine battalion, complete with APCs, tanks, and drop ships.
Tactical Staging Areas: Forward command centers allow real-time mission coordination for Marine officers.
Barracks & Supply Storage: Equipped with long-term housing for extended planetary operations.
Planetary Landing Gear: Unlike standard carriers, the CVN-2018 is built to land on planetary surfaces, turning it into an armored firebase when necessary.
Operational History
The Indomitable is more than a ship—it’s a declaration of war. Wherever it arrives, enemy forces know that resistance is futile. From urban sieges to coastal landings and desert warfare, this war machine ensures United Systems Marine Corps forces are delivered, sustained, and victorious.

"When the Indomitable darkens the sky, the battle is already won."
