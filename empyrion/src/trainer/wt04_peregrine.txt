Woflcorp WT-04 "Peregrine" Advanced Training Fighter
"Precision Through Discipline."

Overview
The Woflcorp WT-04 "Peregrine" is a next-generation atmospheric and low-orbit training fighter, designed to provide an unparalleled piloting experience for cadets, security forces, and private contractors. Built with agility in mind, it bridges the gap between a traditional trainer and a fully combat-ready interceptor. While primarily used for flight and combat training, the Peregrine is more than capable of real-world engagements, featuring an optimized airframe, redundant safety systems, and modular weapons hardpoints.

Design & Performance
The WT-04 Peregrine boasts a sleek, delta-wing airframe with twin stabilizers for high-speed maneuverability in both atmosphere and vacuum. Its reinforced fuselage offers a balance between lightweight construction and resilience, utilizing a composite of armored plating and aerodynamically tuned surfaces. With triple-vector thruster arrays, the Peregrine maintains exceptional agility even at high speeds, allowing pilots to master advanced combat maneuvers without compromising safety.

VTOL Capable – Full hover functionality for smooth landings and tight urban maneuvering.
High G-Force Tolerance – Reinforced cockpit dampens excessive G-forces, preventing pilot blackouts during high-speed engagements.
Multi-Role Flight Training – Configurable avionics allow for combat, reconnaissance, and advanced tactical exercises.
Cockpit & Systems
The Peregrine’s cockpit is built for both solo and dual-pilot configurations, offering a rear instructor seat for supervised training or AI-assisted flight. The avionics suite features holographic HUD elements, real-time targeting assistance, and AI combat scenario simulation.

Fully Transparent Cockpit Design – Maximum visibility for both training and live combat situations.
Integrated Instructor Override – Allows instructors to take control in emergency situations.
Adaptive Targeting Systems – Uses onboard radar and tactical uplink to simulate real combat engagements.
Weapon Loadout & Defensive Capabilities
While the Peregrine is primarily a trainer, it does not lack firepower. The modular hardpoints can accommodate various ballistic and energy-based weapons, allowing pilots to familiarize themselves with different armament types. Standard loadouts include:

Dual Gatling Cannons – Standard for target practice and live fire training.
Hardpoint-Adaptable Wing Mounts – Can support guided missiles or EMP disruptors for electronic warfare exercises.
Flare & Chaff Dispensers – Designed for evasive countermeasures in real-world engagements.
Operational Use
Used by planetary defense forces, corporate security divisions, and elite pilot training programs, the WT-04 Peregrine has become the gold standard in atmospheric combat training. Its balance of speed, agility, and versatility makes it a preferred choice for cadet pilots learning advanced combat tactics before transitioning to full-scale interceptors or multi-role fighters.

Fleet Training Exercises – Simulated combat missions for pilot development.
Escort & Patrol Duties – Fast-response capability for outer colony defense.
Private Sector Security – Deployed by private contractors in high-risk planetary zones.
Final Notes
The Peregrine isn’t just a trainer—it’s a rite of passage for aspiring combat pilots. Designed for both precision and adaptability, it remains a respected fighter even outside the training field. Whether leading rookies into their first dogfight or assisting elite squadrons in tactical drills, the WT-04 Peregrine ensures that every pilot under its canopy emerges faster, deadlier, and ready for the battlefield.
