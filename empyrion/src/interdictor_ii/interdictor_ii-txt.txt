The woflcorp. interdictor ii is a light 1 man interceptor and fighter craft designed to work with alpha 10 requirements. The ship is at 55k CPU use and requires unlock level of 20.

========================================================


Survival features

- Inside it has no cargo storage but redundant ammo controller could be converted to a small amount of storage if needed.
- ammo storage is both large [? volume] and split amongst two redundant containers so reducing the chance of damage disarming the vessel.
- also present are o² and a detector.


Offense and Defense

- The ship comes with 2 137mm homing missile launchers and 4 pulse lasers.
- There is an armoured cockpit sunk low into the front for maximum streamlining and also making it somewhat harder to hit.
- Hull material is... engines - inspired by the infamous woflcorp. I/EAS, the ships main strength comes from it's components not a separate structure, encouraging both strength and lightweight results.
- this on the other hand does have a pure carbon fibre monocoque hull existing only to channnel air for streamlining purposes, providing little defence in the form of armour against most ship mounted weapons.
- the only exception to this is the exposed underbelly of the ship where a skin made of separate sathium/steel plating as ablative armour is present to protect the otherwise vulnerable core and RCS systems.
- The ship is designed to take some hits and does part seperation/redundacy but mostly achieves this ability to soak up any damage via a shiled generator and associated components.


Performance

- all engine design - extreme lightweight carbon fibre highly streamlined hull design saves weight immensely - engines attach to engines with only heavy sathium steel protecting the core systems.
- both the carbon fibre skin and ablative armour around core are extremely aerodynamic, shaping reduces drag.
- greatly reduced fuel consumption results allowing gretaer ranage, airtime and also room for pentaxid and ammo.
