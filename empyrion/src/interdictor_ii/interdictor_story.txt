Woflcorp. Military & Industrial: Interdictor II - Shadow in the Sky

Log Entry: Deep Orbit Patrol | Sector Rho-17 | Outpost Zeta-9

Pilot: Lt. Cas ValenUnit: Interdictor IIMission: Enemy Intercept & Tactical Air Superiority

The silence of deep space was deceptive.

Lt. Cas Valen knew better than to trust the stillness. The Interdictor II coasted in low-power mode, its carbon fiber monocoque hull blending seamlessly with the void. Radar-absorbing materials and its stealth configuration meant that unless someone was actively scanning for heat trails, it was practically a ghost.

Valen flicked his targeting systems to passive scan. Somewhere beyond the asteroid belt, a Woflcorp. convoy was ferrying high-value research assets. And somewhere even farther out, something was hunting them.

The Hunt Begins

A soft chime. Contact.

Valen’s HUD flickered—three inbound bogeys, vectoring fast. Their signatures pegged them as strike drones, likely running autonomous kill orders. Cheap. Disposable. Dangerous.

He exhaled.

“Alright, let’s see how fast you are.”

The Interdictor II’s engines roared to life. The moment its primary thrust system engaged, Valen switched from stealth to full pursuit mode. The ship responded like a living thing—its engine-on-engine frame providing pure acceleration, extreme maneuverability, and a flight response that made it an absolute predator in the void.

First Strike

Valen set his approach vector. The strike drones hadn’t detected him yet. Their focus remained on the convoy’s projected path, assuming they had the element of surprise.

They didn’t.

A single flick of the throttle and the Interdictor II tore through the black like a dart, closing the gap at insane speed. Valen’s targeting AI synced lock-on signals, feeding real-time trajectories for his 137mm homing missiles.

He pulled the trigger. Two missile locks.

A brief whoosh and two bright contrails lanced forward.

Direct hits.

The first drone detonated into a cloud of molten debris, its weak shields offering no resistance. The second spun violently out of control before a pulse laser burst finished the job.

The third drone adjusted course. It knew it was being hunted.

Dogfight in the Dark

“Alright,” Valen muttered. “Let’s see what you’ve got.”

The drone twisted, shifting into erratic flight patterns to shake him. It was smart, but not smart enough.

Valen flipped the Interdictor II into a high-G roll, pulling a sudden, brutal turn that should’ve ripped lesser ships apart.

The ship handled it flawlessly.

The drone banked, trying to lead him into a debris cluster—bad move. Valen cut power for 0.8 seconds, letting the ship drift in silence before reigniting full thrust. The drone lost track.

That was all he needed.

A triple pulse laser burst smashed into its rear stabilizers. The drone died mid-burn, spiraling away into the void.

Extraction & Evac

Valen checked his scopes. The convoy was clear.

“Control, this is Interdictor Lead. Targets eliminated. Your package is safe.”

A response came through, laced with static. “Copy, Interdictor. Nice work.”

Valen smirked, rolling the Interdictor II into a low-energy drift.

“Like I said,” he murmured to himself, watching the wreckage fade into the void, “fast enough?”

Not even close.

End Transmission.
