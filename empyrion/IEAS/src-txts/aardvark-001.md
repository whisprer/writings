LOG 01 - The Mining Rig
Timestamp: 04:23 UTC
Subject: Compact Mining Rig 04 Operational Deployment

The barren, iron-laced highlands of Inzaka-4 were black rock and jagged, wind-scoured cliffs—exactly the kind of place woflcorp’s Compact Mining Rig 04 was built for. It had been running here for six planetary cycles, humming in the howling void, feeding autominers in absolute safety. The automated turrets had already neutralized six wave attacks from local raiders.

A transmission cut through the static, encrypted and tightband:

"Eyes up. Redmist inbound. We’re calling in the Aardvark."

An escort SV was detected on long-range scanners—not friendly. The rig’s AI flicked turret protocols from "standby" to "engagement-ready".

They were coming to steal the ore.

Not today.

LOG 02 - The Levit8-AERO™ Makes Contact
Timestamp: 04:31 UTC
Subject: Levit8-AERO Insert/Extract Deployment

In the low-orbit blackness, cutting through the upper ionosphere at brutal speeds, was the Levit8-AERO™ I/EAS—woflcorp’s original Insert/Extract Assault Shuttle. It was moving too fast for tracking locks.

The escort SV had no idea what was coming.

"Tag confirmed. Engaging intercept."

The Levit8-AERO™ didn’t decelerate—it skipped across the thin atmosphere like a tungsten round, using hybrid thrusters to burn straight into the enemy’s blind spot.

Two seconds later, the sky lit up in streaks of railgun fire.

LOG 03 - Enter the Aardvark
Timestamp: 04:35 UTC
Subject: I/EAS-Aardvark Variant Deploys

The original Levit8-AERO™ was fast. The Aardvark was unstoppable.

Beneath the war-torn sky, ground teams were still pinned down at the mining rig. That’s when the Aardvark ripped into the atmosphere like a reentry missile, its thickened armor plating glowing white-hot, its modular turreted weapons already locking targets before its landing struts even deployed.

The Redmist raiders didn’t have time to adjust.

"Patching through target confirmation—no friendlies within blast radius."

One plasma barrage later, the entire enemy squad vanished from the hillside.

The Aardvark’s twin side hatches slammed open, exfil units poured out, reinforcing the mining rig before a second attack could be mustered.

LOG 04 - Extraction Complete
Timestamp: 04:42 UTC
Subject: woflcorp. Dominance Confirmed

The Levit8-AERO peeled off from its burning target, leaving only debris spiraling into the lower atmosphere.

On the ground, the Compact Mining Rig 04 stood untouched, still running its autominer in total operational status.

And the Aardvark?

Already lifting off with its team, heading for its next mission.

Because woflcorp. never stops.
