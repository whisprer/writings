═════════════
"The Slipstream Job"
═════════════
Some heists need a plan.
Some heists need firepower.
This one needed a miracle.

The last job on Veskar-IV had barely cooled before woflcorp. had another mission lined up—this time, off-world and with far fewer pretenses.

No fake dino safari.
No illusion of a legitimate operation.
Just a straight-up theft in the void.

The target?
A prototype Slipstream Drive, classified tech designed to break through high-tier interdiction fields and skip through folded-space lanes—a game-changer for smugglers, warlords, and, of course, woflcorp.

The problem?
It was currently locked inside a UHG (Unified Helios Guard) dreadstation, deep within a secured orbital drydock.

This was not a “break in, grab the loot, and run” kind of job.

This was a full-scale infiltration, distraction, and extraction mission.

And for that, woflcorp. had assembled their best bastards—the Shogun, the Gemini/Falcon smuggler duo, and the mercenary dreadnought Hexreaper.

═════════════
Phase One: The "Friendly" Approach
═════════════
"Let me get this straight," Dez Arvan, pilot of the Gemini, said over the shared comms. "We're just flying up to a UHG restricted station and pretending we’re supposed to be there?"

"Exactly," came the smooth, almost smug voice of Kex, the "negotiator" for woflcorp.

"That’s it? That’s the plan?"

Kex laughed. "No, no, that’s just the beginning of the plan."

From a distance, the woflcorp. fleet approached the station under the guise of delivering a custom retrofit package—something high-end corporate fleets did all the time.

The Hexreaper stayed at a safe distance, its massive frame too conspicuous, while the Gemini and Falcon, running on spoofed clearance codes, docked on the outer pylons as "supply runners."

And the Shogun?

It sat quietly, latched onto the underbelly of the station, running on stealth power, waiting for its cue.

This was the "friendly" part.

It wouldn’t last long.

═════════════
Phase Two: A Well-Placed Sabotage
═════════════
With the station’s security systems already under passive subversion, Kex and his crew moved inside, posing as independent retrofit engineers—their cover so airtight that they even made a few actual repairs just for authenticity.

Meanwhile, the Gemini and Falcon offloaded "supplies"—weapons, hacking tools, and a very large quantity of explosive charges disguised as engineering kits.

A few well-placed "maintenance overrides" and suddenly, the docking station’s defense grid started showing "routine malfunctions."

It was an old trick, but UHG personnel were overworked and underpaid, so it worked like a charm.

By the time station command noticed the pattern of failures, it was too late.

Siv Korr’s voice crackled over the comms. "Alright, friends, time for Phase Three."

═════════════
Phase Three: "We Have A Problem"
═════════════
The Hexreaper powered up its interdiction disruptor, simulating a massive incoming slipstream fluctuation—a catastrophic failure scenario that could tear a station apart if left unchecked.

Instant panic.

Station personnel scrambled to re-align containment systems, unaware that the real danger was already inside.

The Shogun, previously in stealth mode, detached from the station underbelly, powered up, and made its move.

Its crew of specialists stormed the primary lab, securing the Slipstream Drive without firing a single shot.

And just like that, the station’s most valuable prototype vanished.

But now came the hard part.

═════════════
Phase Four: A Loud and Messy Exit
═════════════
The UHG station finally caught on.

Alarms blared.
Docking bays went into lockdown mode.
Gunships powered up.

But Woflcorp had anticipated this.

The "malfunctioning" systems?
They weren’t just sabotaged.

They were rigged.

Before security teams could even organize, massive coolant ruptures erupted across the station, knocking power out in half the sectors.

The Gemini and Falcon immediately broke off, their sleek forms weaving through incoming fighter craft.

The Shogun, running full burn, punched straight through a docking pylon, sending a section of the station spiraling into deep space.

And the Hexreaper?

It jumped in, guns hot.

Within seconds, its disruptor arrays scrambled every targeting system on the station, while its broadside plasma batteries lit up pursuing fighters like fireworks.

═════════════
Phase Five: The Uncatchable Fleet
═════════════
By the time the UHG dreadstation called for reinforcements, the Shogun had already vanished—slingshotting off a gas giant’s gravity well at relativistic speeds, using the very same Slipstream Drive it had stolen to make its escape.

The Hexreaper engaged its own interdictor, dropping into deep-space drift mode—nearly undetectable.

And the Gemini and Falcon?
Already off-grid.

By the time UHG reinforcements arrived, there was nothing left to chase.

Just an embarrassed station commander, an exploded docking bay, and a very large hole where a billion-credit prototype used to be.

═════════════
Epilogue: Woflcorp Always Wins
═════════════
Somewhere, aboard the Hexreaper, Kex leaned back, sipping an imported drink that probably cost as much as a fighter-class engine.

"Smoothest job yet," Siv Korr muttered, feet up on the table.

"You call that smooth?" Dez scoffed. "We literally blew up half a station, and the Shogun flew through a structural collapse."

"Yeah," Kex grinned, swirling his drink. "And we got away with it, didn't we?"

The room erupted in laughter.

Outside, the fleet drifted in the void, another mission complete, another payday secured, and another mystery stolen in woflcorp.'s name.

What was the Slipstream Drive?

Why did woflcorp. want it so badly?

No one was asking.

Because in the smuggling world, curiosity gets you spaced.

And woflcorp. always wins.

Mission Status: Successful.
Casualties: Mostly UHG pilots. Some docking bays. A lot of poor bastards with bad luck.
Loot: One classified Slipstream Drive. One destroyed station’s worth of priceless scrap.
