woflcorp. Special Operations Log – “Black Aardvarks & Ghost Wolves”
A Crossover Operation Featuring the I/EAS Aardvark, Levit8-AERO™, Compact Mining Rig 04, and the Original Spec Ops Division Insert/Extract Assault Shuttle
Mission Briefing – The Target: Wolf's Fang
Location: Derelict Sector Gamma-19, Near the Rift Edges
Objective: Recover black-market Levit8-AERO™ technology before it reaches Redmist warlords
Secondary Objective: Secure vital resources from woflcorp.'s compromised Compact Mining Rig 04
Risk Level: Extreme

LOG 01 - When Ghosts Meet Predators
Timestamp: 03:47 UTC
Subject: Unregistered Levit8-AERO™ Detected

"Got eyes on it. That’s one of ours—except it ain’t."

Inside the cockpit of the I/EAS Aardvark, flight lead Renna "Hex" Vostek zoomed in on her HUD. The unmistakable outline of a Levit8-AERO™ I/EAS hovered over a scarred asteroid field.

But something was wrong.

The hull markings were stripped—no woflcorp insignia, no ID beacon. The engine emissions didn’t match baseline specs. Someone had been tinkering with stolen woflcorp tech.

"That’s the ghost we’re hunting."

The original spec-ops Levit8-AERO™, now corrupted and running as a black-market warship, was preparing to dock with a hijacked woflcorp Compact Mining Rig 04 on the asteroid’s surface.

The Aardvark crew wasn’t about to let that happen.

LOG 02 - Burning Hard, Dropping Fast
Timestamp: 03:51 UTC
Subject: Aardvark Goes Hot

The Aardvark was never built for subtlety.

Unlike the Levit8-AERO™, which operated like a whisper on the battlefield, the Aardvark was a goddamn avalanche.

It dropped out of orbit at a brutal velocity, airfoils deploying at the last second, scattering dust and debris like a meteor impact.

The hijacked Compact Mining Rig 04's turrets activated, tracking the descent—then suddenly went dark.

"Rig’s AI still recognizes woflcorp. firmware," said co-pilot Havik.
"We just got override access. She’s ours again."

They had the rig.

But they still had to deal with the rogue Levit8-AERO™.

LOG 03 - The Old Wolf Still Bites
Timestamp: 03:54 UTC
Subject: A Ghost vs. An Apex Hunter

The stolen Levit8-AERO™ moved like a wraith, darting through asteroid cover, trying to slip into an escape corridor before woflcorp could lock it down.

But Renna "Hex" Vostek wasn’t about to let a ghost get away.

The Aardvark flipped hard, thrusters screaming, and punched forward.

"I see you, little ghost."

The rogue Levit8-AERO™ veered, banking impossibly fast. A lesser ship would’ve spun out, but this was Levit8 tech—it adapted, recovered, and twisted through space like a predator evading a kill shot.

Hex grinned.

"Alright. Let’s see if you can handle this."

She cut main thrust, let inertia flip the Aardvark belly-up, and opened fire with quad-linked railguns.

The asteroid field ignited with kinetic slugs ripping through rock and void.

The rogue pilot had to break cover.

And that’s when the original woflcorp. Insert/Extract Assault Shuttle—operating in stealth—struck from behind.

A blue-white flash as its EMP turret discharged—the stolen Levit8-AERO™ went dark in seconds.

"Nice of you to join the fight," Hex smirked, her sensors confirming the capture.
"Like old times," came the calm reply over comms.

The original I/EAS had been shadowing the battle from the start—waiting for the perfect moment to strike.

LOG 04 - woflcorp. Cleans House
Timestamp: 04:05 UTC
Subject: Stolen Technology Reclaimed

The rogue Levit8-AERO™ was disabled, locked into a magnetic tow from the Aardvark, ready to be pulled back for decryption, reverse-engineering, and complete system cleansing.

The Compact Mining Rig 04 was back under woflcorp. control, its autominer spitting out refined sathium like it never missed a cycle.

And as for the Aardvark and the original Insert/Extract Assault Shuttle?

They left the battlefield together, two wolves in the black—one old, one new.

Different breeds. Same legendary bite.
