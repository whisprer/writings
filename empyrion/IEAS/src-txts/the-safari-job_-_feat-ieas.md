═════════════
"The Safari Job"
═════════════
Deep-space smuggling. Military black-ops. Woflcorp. Special projects.
These worlds usually didn't mix.

But sometimes, just sometimes, they had to.

The woflcorp. I/EAS-3lectron "Shogun", the Gemini/Falcon smuggler duo, and the mercenary dreadnought Hexreaper were about to cross paths in an operation so convoluted it could only be the product of mutual distrust and corporate misdirection.

On paper, it was simple:

A high-rolling VIP wanted an exclusive dino-hunting safari on Veskar-IV, an underdeveloped backwater planet notorious for apex predator megafauna. Woflcorp had hired independent smugglers—the Gemini and Falcon—as support craft, citing “cargo retrieval needs” during the trip. And, because this was woflcorp., they insisted on bringing their own security team, meaning the Shogun was getting dropshipped in from the Hexreaper.

Total overkill for a hunting trip.
Which meant, of course, that it wasn’t really a hunting trip.

═════════════
Arrival at Veskar-IV: "Just a Routine Expedition."
═════════════
Veskar-IV’s dense jungle sprawled endlessly beneath the descending fleet.

The Hexreaper, a monstrous black-edged mercenary dreadnought, loomed in high orbit—its weapons deactivated under local authority scrutiny. Suspicious eyes watched from orbital control stations, tracking its every move. Meanwhile, the Gemini and Falcon, nimble, heavily-modded smuggler vessels, swept ahead of the Shogun, performing routine scouting runs of the dino-infested wilderness.

Officially, they were escorting an unnamed "VIP client" who had paid top credits for the rarest of game: the Veskar Apex Drakollis—a 14-meter-long, armored, heat-seeking carnivore.

Unofficially?

The "VIP" was a fabrication, and the actual mission involved covert asset retrieval, something buried in the jungle ruins beneath the hunting ground.

"Alright, final descent coming up," Siv Korr called over the shared comms from the Shogun's cockpit. "Hope your boys brought big guns, ‘cause I hear these dinos bite real hard."

"Oh, we did," came a response from Tayren Vos, the Falcon’s co-pilot. "Not sure if we’re gonna need ‘em for the hunt or for this mystery job we all agreed to without askin’ questions."

There was laughter over the comms. No one in the smuggling world ever asked questions.

But they all had their suspicions.

═════════════
On the Ground: Guns, Lies, and Apex Predators
═════════════
The moment the Shogun landed, the fake safari went into full swing—just enough to keep up appearances.

The smuggler crews set up camp, rolling out legitimate-looking supplies—high-powered rifles, night-vision scopes, tracking drones. Someone even erected a fire pit. They looked like a ragtag bunch of big-game thrill-seekers with more money than common sense.

Of course, the real game wasn't Drakollis dinos.

Beneath the jungle, hidden deep in the ruins, was a vault of unknown origin—something woflcorp. was willing to pull every string to get their hands on. Something even the smugglers hadn’t been briefed on.

"Alright, let's get to it," Siv muttered as he jumped down from the Shogun. He adjusted the old ballistic vest slung over his flight suit and tapped a data pad. "Kex, what's the first site?"

Kex, standing next to one of the smugglers—Dez Arvan from the Gemini—brought up a holographic terrain map.

"Coordinates line up with an old crash site—wreckage from a pre-collapse exploration vessel. Looks like the ship's power core is still sending out trace signals. If what we're looking for is in the wreckage, we won’t be the only ones interested."

That was putting it lightly.

Because someone else had already arrived.

═════════════
Uninvited Guests: The Black Dagger Division
═════════════
The Black Daggers were woflcorp.'s deniable assets, off-the-books hit squads—notorious for killing first and not even bothering to ask questions later.

The ruins had motion signatures—half a dozen stealthed strike craft parked in the tree line.

"Looks like we ain't the only ones ‘on safari’ today," Tayren muttered, flicking the safety off her sidearm.

Kex sighed, rubbing his forehead. "Why does every mission turn into a last-minute knife fight?"

"Because," Siv grinned, cracking his knuckles, "we work for woflcorp., and they love a good backstab."

The Gemini and Falcon split to flank the enemy, their VTOL thrusters kicking up dust as they went low and fast, setting up sniper overwatch positions. The Shogun, meanwhile, stayed put, its weapons hot but silent—playing the part of an oblivious civilian dropship.

Black Dagger had no idea they were about to be outmaneuvered.

═════════════
Gunfire, Betrayal, and the Safest Bet in the Galaxy
═════════════
The fight kicked off fast.

Tayren opened with a sniper shot clean through an officer’s visor, dropping him mid-scan. Siv and Kex flanked left, sweeping their way through the jungle, pulse rifles spitting controlled bursts.

The Black Daggers weren’t amateurs, but they were outnumbered and outgunned.

The smugglers played dirty, using the terrain to bait and trap their opponents, while the Shogun—fully shielded—drew enemy fire.

It ended as quickly as it began.

"All clear," Dez called over the comms, standing over the final Black Dagger officer, who bled out on the mossy floor.

═════════════
The Vault: What Was It Worth?
═════════════
With the enemy down, they finally breached the ruins.

Inside, buried under decades of rubble, they found the core of the wrecked ship—not just a power unit, but an ancient data vault.

Woflcorp. wanted what was inside—badly enough to set up this entire safari scam just to retrieve it.

Siv glanced at Kex. "You ever get the feeling we don’t wanna know what's inside?"

Kex smirked, tossing him a hard drive. "That's why we don’t ask questions, remember?"

By the time Black Dagger reinforcements arrived, the Shogun was already mid-burn through the atmosphere, Gemini and Falcon in tow, disappearing into the void.

═════════════
Epilogue: "Another Job, Another Secret"
═════════════
Later, aboard the Hexreaper, the debriefing was short.

Woflcorp. had what they came for.

The smugglers got paid.

And, as an added bonus—one of the snipers did manage to tag a Drakollis from orbit.

But that’s another story.

═════════════
Mission Status: Successful? Probably.
Casualties: Some very unlucky Black Dagger personnel.
Loot: One mysterious data vault. One mounted Drakollis skull.
