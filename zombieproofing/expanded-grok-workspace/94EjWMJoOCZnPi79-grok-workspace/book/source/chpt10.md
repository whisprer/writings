# Chapter 10: Power & Endurance — Keep Your Radio Running While Zombies Hunt

## Introduction: Power Is Your Lifeblood

No power means no signal, no hack, no survival. This chapter tackles batteries, power management, alternative energy, and strategies to keep your radio gear alive when the world goes dark.

---

## Battery Types and Selection

- **Lead Acid:** Heavy, cheap, reliable. Perfect for base stations and solar banks.
- **Lithium-Ion (Li-ion):** Lightweight, high energy density, but must be managed carefully.
- **Nickel-Metal Hydride (NiMH):** Cheap and safer than Li-ion but lower capacity.
- **Alkaline:** Great for small handheld devices, non-rechargeable, practical for spares.

---

## Battery Care and Management

- Avoid deep discharge to extend life.
- Use proper chargers and charge controllers.
- Keep batteries at moderate temperatures.
- Monitor voltage and capacity regularly.

---

## Solar Power Systems

- Solar panels: Rigid or flexible, sized to your power needs.
- Charge controllers prevent battery overcharge.
- Batteries store solar energy for nighttime and cloudy days.
- Portable systems useful for mobile survivors.

---

## Power Calculation and Efficiency

- Calculate device power draw in Watts.
- Estimate hours of operation given battery capacity (Ah) and voltage.
- Use power-saving modes and efficient hardware.

---

## Alternative Power Sources

- Hand-crank generators for emergency power.
- Thermoelectric generators using heat differences.
- Vehicle power taps (cigarette lighter, battery clamps).

---

## Survival Tips

- Carry multiple power options and recharge methods.
- Build solar charging outposts for base stations.
- Prep for seasonal variation — winter solar is low, have backups.
