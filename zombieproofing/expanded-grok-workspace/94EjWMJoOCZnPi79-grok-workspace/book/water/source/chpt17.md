Chapter 17: A Morning with Water

The morning does not need a speech. It needs a cup you can explain to a person who was not there when you treated it.

If the tap is running and the authorities are happy, drink the tap, and use the rest of this book as practice and as a rain barrel's manners. If the tap is a rumour, walk the list without improvising.

Sealed water first, for drinking and for anyone small. If that is gone, the water you settled, then boiled for a minute — three if you are high — or dosed with unscented bleach whose percent you can read, then waited out. Roof water only after the first wash has been thrown away, and still treated. Fog water only as the small extra it is, and still treated. Nothing from the flood, nothing from the sprinkler, nothing that smells of a chemical, nothing from the fuel-can-that-is-now-a-jug because it is not.

The container is opaque, or in the dark, food-grade, dated, and closed. The ladle is clean. The note on the barrel says the day and the method in words a stranger can use. If you are pouring for a baby, you already chose boil-and-cool or a sealed bottle, and you did not get creative with iodine.

Gravity can bring it down the hill if the hill is high enough and the stand is a stand. The olla can keep the tomatoes from becoming a drama, and nobody drinks from the olla. The kettle can be hot. The coil on the stove is not your project this year.

## What you tell the next person

Where it came from. What you did. What you did not do. When. Where the bleach is, and what percent the label claims, and that the label wins. Where the fuel for the kettle is, and that the burner lives outdoors or under a real flue. Who must not be given the iodine. Which tap is still "do not drink" because it talks to a well you have not tested.

Then you pour the cup, you put the lid back, and you do not leave the story in your head. Heads are where stories go to become rumours. Paper is cheaper than a second illness.

The dead may walk. The water still needs a boil. The roof still needs its first wash thrown away. The sprinkler pipe still is not a well. None of that is heroic. It is the reason the morning has a drink in it, and the reason the drink does not become the emergency.

Check the lid. Then quiet.
