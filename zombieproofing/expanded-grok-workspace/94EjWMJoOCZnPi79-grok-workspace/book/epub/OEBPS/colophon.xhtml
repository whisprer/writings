Zombieproof Water: How to Keep a Cup When the Tap Goes Quiet was set in Fraunces and Literata. The plates were painted for this edition. The text was prepared from a pile of public-health notes, rainwater manuals, and a foundation draft on off-grid water, then dressed in the voice of the yard. The doses were kept where the notes gave them, and labelled as indicative. Nothing in the arithmetic was promoted to a universal recipe.

Trim: six by nine inches. The author, the dedication, and the publisher are intentionally blank.

If you are holding a file and not a book, print it on paper that can stand a damp bench. Leave the last page near the kettle, not in a drawer. The boil is still due.
