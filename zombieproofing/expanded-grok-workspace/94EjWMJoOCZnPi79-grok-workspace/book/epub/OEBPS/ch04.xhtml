Chapter 4

Fog, If the Hill Is Kind

Some hills spend half their lives inside a cloud that has not committed to being rain. The cloud is still water. It is merely shy, and it arrives as droplets small enough to ignore a gutter: on the order of one to forty microns, which is a way of saying you will not catch them in a bucket by staring.

Fog collection is a fence with manners. Mesh stands across the wind. Droplets hit the filaments, join up until they are heavy enough to be a drip, and run down into a trough. The trough is the whole technology's moment of honesty. If the drip falls back into the dirt, you have built a sculpture.

The cloth the experiments like is Raschel mesh, a polyethylene shade cloth. A single layer is the baseline. Two layers, or a puffier three-dimensional knit, give the droplet more places to change its mind and become liquid. In one set of trials a multi-layer or three-dimensional arrangement gathered more than twice the water of a single sheet — on the order of ninety-nine millilitres against thirty-nine, in that test's little world. That is a ratio, not a promise that your hillside will fill a bath. Yields depend on how often the fog actually visits, how wet it is, and whether you pointed the mesh at the wind or at the view.

Point it at the weather, not the sunset

Face the fence across the prevailing damp wind, not along it. Put it where the cloud meets the ground often, which is usually higher and ruder than the picnic spot. Keep the trough clean. A mesh that sags into a pocket grows algae and a smell, and then you have invented a swamp with ambition.

Fog water is not distilled holy water. It has met air, and air has met birds, dust, and whatever the upwind valley is burning. Treat it before you drink it, the same as roof water. And do not move to a dry ridge because a paper used the number 150 percent. A hundred and fifty percent of a trickle is a slightly more confident trickle.

The zombie will stand in the fog with its mouth open. This is not a design. The mesh is the design. The zombie is the control group.
