Chapter 8

The Kettle Is the Grown-up

If you can boil, boil. It is the method with the fewest opinions about brands.

A rolling boil — big bubbles, the real thing, not a shy shimmer — for one minute is the figure on the emergency cards for killing bacteria, viruses, and parasites, including the cysts that chlorine sulks at. Above about six thousand five hundred feet, where the water boils cooler because the air is thinner, those same cards say three minutes. The research notes sometimes draw the line nearer five thousand feet or a thousand metres. Mountains are not a place to economise. If you are high enough to wonder, boil for three.

Let it cool, with a lid, in a clean container. Do not add ice made from water you have not met. Do not stick a finger in "to test it," unless the finger has a retirement plan.

Boiled water tastes flat because you drove the dissolved air out of it. Pour it back and forth between two clean vessels, or leave it an hour, or add a pinch of salt to a litre if you want it to taste like water instead of like a lecture. That is cosmetics. It is not a second treatment.

What the kettle refuses

Boiling does not remove metals, salts, fuels, or most chemical pollutants. It can make them a little stronger, because some of the water leaves as steam and the contaminant stays. If the source was a ditch of manure, the kettle is your friend. If the source was a ditch of diesel, the kettle is a concentrator. Walk away.

Boiling also does not protect the water after it cools. A clean cup can be re-dirtied by a dirty ladle, a thirsty pet, or a lid left off in a room of flies. The boil is a moment. Storage, in the next part of the book, is the rest of the week.

For a baby, if sealed water is gone, boiled and cooled is the path this book will stand next to. Do not boil it down to a syrup. Do not add bleach to formula "to be sure." Follow the formula tin and a clinician if you can get either. Cooling matters: a scald is not a purification step.

Fuel for the boil is part of the method. A kettle on a stove indoors is ordinary life. A charcoal burner in a closed room is the engine book's carbon monoxide, wearing a saucepan. Do the burning where the exhaust can leave. The zombie will boil a pan in a cupboard. You will not.

One minute. Three if the hill is high. Lid on. Then it is water you can explain.
