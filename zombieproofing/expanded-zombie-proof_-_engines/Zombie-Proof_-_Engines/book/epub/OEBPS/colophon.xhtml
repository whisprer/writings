Zombieproof Engines: How to Keep the Wheels Turning was set in Fraunces and Literata. The plates were drawn for this edition. The text was prepared from a foundation draft on engines, fuels, and revival, then dressed in the voice of the yard. The numbers were kept where the draft gave them, and labelled as indicative. Nothing in the arithmetic was promoted to a universal recipe.

Trim: six by nine inches. The author, the dedication, and the publisher are intentionally blank.

If you are holding a file and not a book, print it on paper that can stand a workbench. Leave the last page near the machine, not in a drawer. The oil is still due.
