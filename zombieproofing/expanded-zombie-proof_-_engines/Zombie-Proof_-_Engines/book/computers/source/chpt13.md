# Zombieproof Computers: How to Keep a Mind When the Grid Is Gone

Chapter 13: Cooling and Enclosure

Now that you’ve mastered brainpower, memory, and power supply, it’s time to keep your zombieproof computer physically safe from the elements and overheating—both of which can kill even the toughest hardware quicker than a zombie bite.

This chapter covers practical cooling techniques, enclosure design principles, and durability hacks tailored for survival scenarios, so your machine keeps firing on all cylinders when the undead horde comes knocking.

Why Cooling Matters
Digital circuits generate heat due to current flow through millions of transistors and logic gates.

Excess heat can cause timing errors, degrade components, and shorten electronics lifespan.

Efficient heat dissipation is vital for reliable operation, especially in confined spaces or hot environments.

Basic Cooling Techniques
Natural convection: Design enclosures with ventilation holes allowing airflow.

Passive cooling: Use heatsinks—metal fins that draw heat away from chips.

Active cooling: Small fans powered by your system, moving air to remove heat.

Thermal interface materials: Use thermal paste or pads to improve contact between chips and heatsinks.

Enclosure Design Principles
Use durable, non-conductive materials (plastic, wood treated for flame resistance).

Make it modular for easy access to boards and components for repair.

Protect from dust, water, and shock with sealed or padded compartments.

Include mounting points for cables, sensors, and peripherals securely.

Survival Enhancements
Use surge protectors and power conditioners against erratic power sources.

Implement humidity and temperature sensors to alert for dangerous conditions.

Consider camouflage enclosures for stealth in hostile territories.

Survival Project: Assembling a Durable Enclosure
Survey available materials — repurposed casings, wood boxes, rugged plastics.

Design airflow paths balancing protection and cooling.

Integrate power and interface pass-throughs with sealed connectors.

Test enclosure with temperature sensors under extended operation.

A well-cooled, well-protected zombieproof computer will outlast threats both natural and undead, empowering you with dependable tech for survival.
