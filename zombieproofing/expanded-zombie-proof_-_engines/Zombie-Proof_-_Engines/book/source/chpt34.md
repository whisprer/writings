Ham radio is a world of traditions and gatekeeping, but in a zombie apocalypse, everyone is welcome. The Basics of Ham Radio
Ham radio uses a variety of technologies to transmit data over radio waves. From Morse code to streaming video, ham radio keeps you connected and informed. In a zombie apocalypse, ham radio is your lifeline to the outside world.

Building Your Own Ham Radio Transceiver
You don’t need a fancy transceiver to get started. With a few scavenged parts, you can build your own ham radio transceiver. Start with a ham radio module, which can be found in old radios, TVs, or even car stereos. Pair it with a microcontroller, and you’ve got the brains of your transceiver. Add a power supply, and you’re ready to roll.

The Protocols of Apocalypse Ham Radio
Ham radio uses a variety of protocols, including CW, SSB, and FM. These standards ensure that your transceiver can transmit data from any broadcaster, no matter who built it. In a zombie apocalypse, interoperability is key. You never know what kind of tech you’ll find in a scavenged transceiver, so stick to the standards.

Security in the Apocalypse
Security is a major concern when the world is falling apart. Ham radio uses encryption and authentication to protect your data. In a zombie apocalypse, you’ll want to use the strongest encryption available. Look for systems that support AES-128 or higher, and always keep your software up to date. If you’re building your own system, make sure to implement secure key management and regular firmware updates.

The Future of Apocalypse Ham Radio
As the world rebuilds, ham radio will become even more important. With the rise of digital broadcasting and decentralized communication, you might find yourself building your own transceiver from scratch. Whether you’re using CW, SSB, FM, or something entirely new, the future of apocalypse ham radio is bright.
