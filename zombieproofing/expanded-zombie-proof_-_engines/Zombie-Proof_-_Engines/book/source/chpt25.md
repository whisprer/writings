# Chapter 25: Practical AI Projects for Radio Enthusiasts and Survivalists

## Introduction: Turning Theory into Survival Tools

Explore concrete AI applications that enhance your radio projects for detection, decoding, and network optimization—empowering your survival communications.

---

## Project 1: AI-Based Signal Classifier

- Train a simple neural network to classify common modulation types (AM, FM, FSK, PSK).
- Use labeled signal datasets or capture your own with SDR.
- Employ Python libraries like TensorFlow or PyTorch.
- Integrate classifier with GNU Radio for real-time signal sorting.

---

## Project 2: Automated Morse Code Decoder

- Use recurrent neural networks (RNNs) or CNNs to decode Morse tones amid noise.
- Preprocess raw audio signals with bandpass filters.
- Train model on Morse audio samples; test with field recordings.
- Output decoded text automatically to aid messaging.

---

## Project 3: Adaptive Frequency Hopping Controller

- Implement reinforcement learning agent to manage frequency hopping sequences.
- Optimize for minimal interference and maximum stealth.
- Deploy on embedded AI platforms for real-time control.

---

## Project 4: AI-Powered Noise Reduction

- Train AI models for spectral noise suppression in radio signals.
- Improve weak, distorted signals from hostile sources.
- Integrate with SDR receivers for cleaner audio and data.

---

## Tools and Resources

- Jupyter notebooks for prototyping.
- Open datasets like RadioML for training.
- Collaborate via open-source clubs or forums.

---

## Survival Tip: Start with Small Proofs of Concept

- Validate ideas on simple hardware before full deployment.
- Share and test with your survival network.
- Incrementally add AI complexity to maintain reliability.
