# Chapter 19: Emergency Communications Setups, Portable Repeaters, and Low-Tech Hacks

## Introduction: Stay Connected When It Counts

In the apocalypse, quick, reliable communication saves lives. This chapter explores setting up emergency comms, building portable repeaters, and leveraging low-tech hacks for reliable signal coverage even in the toughest conditions.

---

## Emergency Comms Essentials

- Keep radios pre-programmed with emergency frequencies.
- Use simplex and duplex channels to maximize reach.
- Have backup power solutions plugged in and ready.
- Establish check-in procedures and schedules.

---

## Portable Repeaters

- Extend radio range using battery-powered or solar-enabled repeaters.
- Use simple cross-band repeaters or dual-radio setups.
- Place repeaters on high ground or vehicles for optimum coverage.
- Design repeaters with fail-safes and remote control features.

---

## Low-Tech Hacks to Boost Coverage

- Use natural elevation (hills, buildings) to increase signal line-of-sight.
- Construct wire antennas on trees, poles, or improvised masts.
- Reuse old satellite dishes or metal surfaces as reflectors.
- Employ signal relays with trusted operators to hop messages.

---

## Improvised Communication Methods

- Use flags, lights, or sound (whistles, drums) for silent or low-power signaling.
- Signal mirrors for optical communication during daytime.
- Pre-arranged visual or audio codes to convey critical info.

---

## Survival Tips

- Regularly test all equipment in real conditions.
- Train with your emergency network to refine protocols.
- Maintain simple, clear communication to avoid confusion.

---

## Sample Setup: Quick Field Repeater

- Two handheld radios with cross-band repeat programmed.
- Portable solar panel and battery pack.
- Lightweight tripod for antenna and repeater mounting.
