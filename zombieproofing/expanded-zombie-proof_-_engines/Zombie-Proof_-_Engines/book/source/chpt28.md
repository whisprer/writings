Chapter 28: Contactless Transactions in the Apocalypse
When the world goes dark and the undead start shuffling, your wallet is the least of your worries—unless it’s a contactless wallet. In a zombie apocalypse, contactless payment and transaction tech can be your lifeline for exchanging supplies, verifying identities, and even bartering with other survivors. Forget about cash and coins; NFC and RFID systems are the new currency of the apocalypse.

The Rise of the Zombie-Proof Wallet
Contactless transactions rely on Near Field Communication (NFC) and Radio-Frequency Identification (RFID) technologies. These systems allow you to make payments or exchange data by simply waving your device near a reader. In a world where touching anything is a gamble, contactless tech is a godsend. Whether you’re trading ammo for canned beans or verifying your identity at a survivor checkpoint, NFC and RFID keep you safe from both zombies and germs.

Building Your Own Contactless Terminal
You don’t need a fancy terminal to get started. With a few scavenged parts, you can build your own contactless payment system. Start with an NFC reader module, which can be found in old smartphones, payment terminals, or even public transit cards. Pair it with a microcontroller like an Arduino or Raspberry Pi, and you’ve got the brains of your system. Add a display for feedback, and you’re ready to roll.

The Protocols of Apocalypse Payments
Contactless transactions use a variety of protocols, including ISO/IEC 14443 and ISO/IEC 18092. These standards ensure that your device can communicate with any reader, no matter who built it. In a zombie apocalypse, interoperability is key. You never know what kind of tech you’ll find in a scavenged terminal, so stick to the standards.

Security in the Apocalypse
Security is a major concern when the world is falling apart. Contactless systems use encryption and authentication to protect your data. In a zombie apocalypse, you’ll want to use the strongest encryption available. Look for systems that support AES-128 or higher, and always keep your software up to date. If you’re building your own system, make sure to implement secure key management and regular firmware updates.

The Future of Apocalypse Payments
As the world rebuilds, contactless transactions will become even more important. With the rise of blockchain and decentralized currencies, you might find yourself trading crypto with other survivors. Whether you’re using NFC, RFID, or something entirely new, the future of apocalypse payments is bright.
