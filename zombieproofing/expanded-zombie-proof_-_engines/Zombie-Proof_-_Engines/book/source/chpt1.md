# Zombieproof Radio: How to Stay Alive on the Airwaves

## Chapter 1: What Is Radio? (And Why Zombies Should Care)

Welcome, post-civ survivor! Whether you're scavenging K-pop t-shirts or building a bunker out of PlayStation cases, you’ll soon discover that *radio* is the real cheat-code for communication in the end times.

### What Counts as "Radio"?

- Radio is electromagnetic energy, part of the spectrum squeezed between sound waves and microwaves.
- It’s how walkie-talkies, satellites, airport scanners, your Bluetooth speaker, and even some microwaves talk to each other.
- Radios work by tossing electromagnetic waves (oscillating electric and magnetic fields) through space at the speed of light.
- Radio = electric and magnetic fields oscillating together
- Frequency (\(f\)), wavelength (\(\lambda\)), and the speed of light (\(c\)) are related by \(\lambda = \frac{c}{f}\).
- Radio is strictly physical, not magic — and sometimes, not even digital!

### A Brief Meme-Ridden History of Radio

- In the 1930s-1940s, radios were living room centerpieces, bigger than most survivors' bugout bags.
- Orson Welles panicked a nation with [translate:La Guerra de los Mundos] (War of the Worlds) — proving radios could out-troll TikTok, even before trolls existed.
- Old-time shows were like audio Netflix, with suspense, laughs, and science fiction all at the twist of a dial[attached_file:3de20966-8916-49fe-9f03-272792a7b96c].

### Why Bother After the End of the World?

- When the grid dies and cell towers are overrun by zombies (or just out-of-service teens), only radio still connects people.
- All modern “wireless” stuff — WiFi, Bluetooth, walkie-talkies, GPS, remote car keys, contactless credit cards — is just radio in a fancier wrapper.
- True apocalypse survivors use radio for:
    - Long-distance memes and squad comms.
    - Weather alerts (to avoid mutant hurricanes).
    - Fox hunting (actually finding hidden transmissions, not animals).
    - Disaster coordination and hacking.
    - Trolling and eavesdropping on the unprepared.

### Key Radio Terms You Actually Need

| Term           | Meaning                                                                                | Example & Meme                              |
|----------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| Frequency (\(f\))   | Swings per second (Hertz, Hz)                                                      | 100 MHz = FM radio                          |
| Wavelength (\(\lambda\)) | Distance between cycle peaks (\(c/f\)), usually in meters                      | 3 meters for 100 MHz                        |
| Modulation     | Encoding information on a wave                                                         | AM, FM, FSK, PSK, all the classics          |
| Bandwidth      | How much information you can pack into a signal                                        | Narrowband walkie-talkie vs. Netflix stream |
| Spectrum       | The whole range of radio frequencies                                                   | "The wild west of waves"                    |
