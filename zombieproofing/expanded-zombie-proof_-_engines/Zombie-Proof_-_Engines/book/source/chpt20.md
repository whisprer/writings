# Chapter 20: Securing Your Comms — Encryption Basics and Stealth Communication Techniques

## Introduction: Guard Your Signals Like Your Life Depends On It

In the apocalypse, eavesdroppers are everywhere—from zombies to hostile survivors. Securing your radio communications with encryption and stealth helps keep your plans and locations secret.

---

## Basics of Encryption

- **Symmetric Encryption:** Same key for encrypting and decrypting (AES, DES).
- **Asymmetric Encryption:** Public/private key pairs (RSA, ECC).
- Use strong keys and rotate them regularly.

---

## Simple Voice and Text Encryption

- Use one-time pads (OTP) for perfect secrecy if implemented correctly.
- Use simple ciphers (Caesar, substitution) for quick scrambling.
- Layer voice scrambling devices or software DSP encryptors.

---

## Digital Encryption in SDR and Radios

- Software DSP can implement secure digital voice codecs.
- Encrypted digital protocols (e.g., AES-256) protect data and voice.
- Key exchange can be manual or via secure public key infrastructure (PKI).

---

## Stealth and Low Probability of Intercept (LPI)

- Frequency hopping spread spectrum (FHSS) to avoid being tracked.
- Use narrowband and low power transmissions.
- Employ burst transmissions or scheduled windows.
- Directional antennas pointed away from likely adversaries.

---

## Cover and Camouflage Techniques

- Use disguising frequencies, mimic ambient signals or noise.
- Employ innocuous cover stories or protocol decoys.
- Vary transmission parameters unpredictably.

---

## Practical Tools and Devices

- Open-source encryption tools: GnuPG, OTR for text.
- Hardware voice scramblers.
- SDR-based LPI implementations.

---

## Survival Tip: Encrypt Your Logs and Key Materials

- Use secure storage for key material and communication logs.
- Avoid revealing cryptographic methods and key schedules in public.
