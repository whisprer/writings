# Chapter 18: Survival Stockpiling and Improvised Materials — Long-Term Maintenance for Zombieproof Radios

## Introduction: Keeping Your Radio Arsenal Ready

Survival in the undead world means your radio gear must be reliable, repairable, and well-stocked for long-term use. This chapter covers smart stockpiling, scavenging parts, and improvising replacements when resources run dry.

---

## Essential Radio Parts to Stockpile

- Common resistors, capacitors (electrolytic, ceramic), and inductors.
- Transistors (BJTs, MOSFETs), diodes (signal and power).
- ICs: Op-amps, audio amplifiers, mixers, voltage regulators.
- Connectors, switches, potentiometers, and fuses.
- Solder, wire, PCB materials, heat shrink tubing.

---

## Scavenging and Improvised Materials

- Salvage parts from discarded electronics: old radios, TVs, car stereos.
- Use copper wire from electrical wiring or scrap for antennas and coils.
- Plastic containers and rubber tubing for enclosures and insulation.
- Tin cans or metal boxes for shielding.
- Batteries from common devices, refurbished solar panels.

---

## Long-term Maintenance Tips

- Clean contacts regularly with alcohol or contact cleaner.
- Protect electronics from moisture and dust.
- Use conformal coatings or varnish on PCBs.
- Rotate stock to avoid part degradation.
- Regularly test batteries and replace weak ones.

---

## Improvised Repair Techniques

- Use jumper wires and twist connections in absence of soldering.
- Create makeshift antennas with random conductive materials.
- Patch damaged cables with foil tape or shrink wrap.
- Improvise heat sinks from aluminum cans or scrap metal.

---

## Building a Basic Survival Repair Kit

- Multitool, soldering iron with spare tips.
- Small selection of passive and active components.
- Replacement cables, connectors, and basic test gear.
- Epoxy, tape, and adhesives.

---

## Example: Scavenged Wire Antenna Build

- Use insulated copper wire wound to approximate length.
- Insulate with electrical tape or tubing.
- Attach to radio via hacked connector or clip.

---

## Survival Mindset

- Stockpile for redundancy to survive extended supply chain collapse.
- Prioritize modular, repair-friendly gear designs.
- Teach maintenance skills within your survival group.
