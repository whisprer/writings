### **1\. Geothermal Buffer Dynamics**

### **2\. Moisture Control &amp; Desiccant Ratios**

Hydrolysis (chemical breakdown driven by water vapor) is the single greatest threat to solid oral tablets, beta-lactams, and surgical wraps[2].

* **Desiccant Selection:** Use **cobalt-free silica gel** (which turns from orange to green when saturated) or **3A/4A molecular sieve desiccants**.

  * *Over-Desiccation Danger:* If relative humidity (RH) inside a sealed pouch drops below **10%**, hard gelatin capsule shells (e.g., Doxycycline or Cephalexin capsules) lose their bound water, become extremely brittle, and crack.
