Zombieproof Medic: How to Keep the Living Living was set in Fraunces and Literata. The plates were painted for this edition. The text was prepared from a civilian field manual already written in this voice, then checked against the austere notes that came with it. Those notes assumed a trained team. Their invasive procedures, drug regimens, and hospital-acquisition plans were not converted into instructions. The public first-aid spine was kept, and labelled as something you still have to learn with your hands.

Trim: six by nine inches. The author, the dedication, and the publisher are intentionally blank. A companion .tex file is included so those blanks can be filled without hunting through a print file.

If you are holding a file and not a book, print the cards in the last chapter and keep them with the kit. The rest of the book is why the cards are short.
