# Editorial source and review notes

Edition: 1.0.0 illustrated editorial edition. Reference check date: 20 October 2026.

This is a complete manuscript and production package, not a clinically signed-off publication. No clinician or pharmacist has reviewed or endorsed it. Do not remove that status statement without obtaining and recording an appropriate independent review. Claims in the supplied acquisition essay were not accepted automatically.

## Source hierarchy

1. Current official UK public first-aid guidance, with the 2025 Resuscitation Council UK adult guidance prioritised where older public pages conflict.
2. NHS public patient information and relevant official FDA/CDC material.
3. Supplied professional Joint Trauma System prolonged-field-care compilation for context on prolonged care, documentation and boundaries. Invasive procedures, professional drug regimens and specialised triage rules were not converted into lay instructions.
4. Supplied 2024 narrative antibiotic review as a discussion of evidence and uncertainty, not authority for household expiry extensions.
5. Supplied START documents for organisational context. These are older training materials, not the book's current resuscitation authority.
6. Supplied ref2.txt as a list of research topics only. Its stock-longevity claims, medicine ranking, sedation claims, sterile-longevity claims, and operational acquisition advice were not reproduced as instructions.

## Specific editorial decisions

- Adult arrest: call 999 immediately for an unresponsive adult, check breathing while connecting, and start CPR if breathing is abnormal. RCUK 2025 takes precedence over older pages that call later.
- Adult arrest on a bed: start there; do not introduce delay by moving to the floor. This follows RCUK 2025 and differs from the older SJA CPR page.
- Infant CPR: the current RCUK public resource uses the two-thumb encircling method. Older SJA pages are retained as secondary background; this edition does not reproduce an older two-finger instruction.
- Open chest wounds: current RCUK lay guidance is used. No improvised airtight seal or needle procedure is taught.
- Tourniquets: commercial devices, labelled instructions, recording time and no lay removal. No invented safe prolonged-evacuation removal interval.
- No lay triage algorithm that declares death or assigns non-treatment. Colour illustrations are fictional, decorative and non-diagnostic.
- No general "5-20 years" or "decades" guarantee for medicines. SLEP is controlled and lot-specific; unknown household stock is not validated by it.
- Insulin requirements depend on the product, container and use status. No blanket room-temperature life or substitution regimen is supplied.
- Adrenaline auto-injectors are not classified as routine refrigerator items.
- No universal quantitative kit minimum, universal waste-pit placement rule, or universal nursing observation/repositioning interval is invented.
- No improvised ORS recipe is supplied; labelled sachet volumes vary. No universal bleach dose is supplied for an unidentified concentration.
- No surgical closure, debridement, extraction, airway cutting, sedation, intravenous fluid, transfusion, catheter insertion or birth procedure is taught.
- Illustrations are narrative, not instructional medical diagrams. The cover, frontispiece and seventeen chapter plates were made for this volume using the built-in image-generation tool with the supplied series as visual reference.
